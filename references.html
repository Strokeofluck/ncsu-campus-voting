  1. 2022: 3,402 at Talley ÷ 13,398 across Wake County × 100 = 25.3918…%, rounded to 25.4%.

These percentages describe where college-age early voters voted. They are not the percentage of students who voted or the percentage growth in ballots. Both county totals include the campus-associated site. Official source files and original counts.

Why early-voting demographics are available before full-election voter history

The article uses NCSBE absentee data, which includes in-person early voting. These voter-level records are released during the voting period and include demographic fields used to aggregate the age groups. This is a separate release from full-election voter history, which documents participation across voting methods after counties process their records.

NCSBE says current voter-history files update weekly and that finalizing all counties can take multiple weeks after an election. As checked November 7, its historical demographic-statistics list did not include the November 2026 general election. That does not prove that no preliminary Election Day demographic records are available anywhere; the article does not claim that.

Sources: Absentee and Provisional Data; 2026 reporter’s primer; Voter History Data — update timing and historical demographic files. The article’s November 4 early-voting snapshot remains preliminary.

3. Routine-route map

The simulation covers all 84 housing buildings. Nine manually traced approximate walking connections supplement the frozen GIS network. The earlier 75 origin route sets are preserved; 198 trips from the nine added origins are appended.

84 origins × 22 destinations = 1,848 pairs. Excluding 84 trips ending at Talley leaves 1,764 trips. 699 (39.6%) pass within 50 meters of Talley’s building footprint; 0 pass within 50 meters of Western Boulevard’s footprint. Both sites use the same eligible trips and distance threshold.

Manually traced connections: method and checks

Pi Kappa Phi connects along Avent Ferry Road to the Walnut Creek Trail GIS gateway. Johnston Hall connects through the visible courtyard path. Western Manor connects through Lilley Court and its visible building access/parking lanes. Waypoints were read from the visible Google Maps coordinate menu on November 9, 2026. Starts use the nearest building edge to each traced access path. These are approximate routes, not surveyed entrances or field-verified public-access guarantees.

Every added connection was checked for a continuous join to the graph and for crossings through GIS building footprints. No segment crosses more than 2.5 meters of a building interior. Existing GIS paths, destination choices, and voting-site footprints are unchanged. Shortest paths are recalculated for the nine added origins; proximity uses full-resolution geometry and map display is simplified by at most 1 meter.

Equal pair weights are not occupancy weights, actual pedestrian traffic or voting probabilities. The manual connections introduce uncertainty, and should not be described as Google’s exported route geometry. The Google distance/time estimates remain a separate input to the housing-distance comparison.
