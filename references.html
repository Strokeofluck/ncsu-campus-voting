Display units and autoplay

  • Miles = meters ÷ 1,609.344.
  • Walking minutes = meters ÷ 80 at the default pace (about 3 mph).
  • Example: 258.4 meters ≈ 0.16 mile ≈ 3.2 minutes.
  • Proximity labels use meters; values below 0.001 mile display as <0.001 mi. Raw calculations retain full precision.

A hypothetical 660-space allotment at Coliseum

Using the GIS-listed totals, an allotment of 660 spaces at Coliseum Deck would account for 30.1% of its inventory and leave 1,532 spaces (69.9%) outside that hypothetical allotment.

  1. 660 ÷ 2,192 × 100 = 30.109…%, rounded to 30.1%.
  2. 2,192 − 660 = 1,532 spaces.
  3. 1,532 ÷ 2,192 × 100 = 69.890…%, rounded to 69.9%.

This allotment is hypothetical. Vacancies and permission to reassign spaces are unverified. The documented McKimmon voter-parking areas do not establish a numerical allotment; current GIS does not reconstruct 2022 parking.
