# Greek west-row access supplement

Shortest connected modeled walk among eligible mapped entrances and available estimated building access points, using the frozen November 1 NC State GIS network with November 10 Bilyeu–Pullen and Greek west-row supplements. Greek parking aisles, a shared access-drive crossing of Varsity Drive, and four estimated rear building-edge connections are included; those rear points are not verified doors. Existing front access remains eligible. Pullen pedestrian paths and bridge connections are supported by OpenStreetMap, Raleigh data and reviewed imagery, including an approximate 24.1 m marked-crossing approach. Public arterial road centerlines are not added. Existing manual housing accesses are retained. Door permissions, field conditions, accessibility, crossing delay and October-specific closures are unverified.

The four rear building-edge points are estimates, not recorded entrances. The aerial shows rear paved aprons, parking aisles and the shared access driveway; it does not establish door permissions or current conditions. Google walking directions for Kappa Alpha Theta on November 10 also exited the rear parking area toward Gorman Street; that check corroborates rear parking connectivity, not a particular door or the model distance. The short Varsity Drive connection follows the shared drive intersection; it is not represented as a marked pedestrian crosswalk.
