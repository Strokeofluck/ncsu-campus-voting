# Frozen NC State GIS evidence

Downloaded November 1, 2026. `catalog.json` and `manifest.json` retain exact query URLs, counts, retrieval time and SHA-256 hashes. Buildings, walking paths, accessible paths and entrances are exact extracts of the existing frozen ZIP. `parking-query.json` preserves the additional 41-feature spatial query; only five named McKimmon/Business Services parking polygons are displayed. Nearby whole-lot outlines do not identify the voter-reserved subsection, capacity available to voters, or current restrictions.

The public original GIS classifications and geometries are retained without relabeling. University source ownership is unchanged. Model-derived routes and estimates remain separately in `data/processed/campus-walk/`.
