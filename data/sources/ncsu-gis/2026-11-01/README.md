# Frozen NC State GIS evidence

Downloaded November 1, 2026. `catalog.json` and `manifest.json` retain exact query URLs, counts, retrieval time and SHA-256 hashes. Buildings, walking paths, accessible paths and entrances are exact extracts of the existing frozen ZIP. `parking-query.json` preserves the 41-feature Western-area spatial query; five McKimmon/Business Services polygons are displayed. `talley-parking-query.json` preserves five Coliseum/Jeter features, all displayed. These current GIS polygons do not identify reserved levels/bays, voter capacity, or fall 2022 parking arrangements. Dated evidence and its limits are in `parking-evidence.json`.

The public original GIS classifications and geometries are retained without relabeling. University source ownership is unchanged. Model-derived routes and estimates remain separately in `data/processed/campus-walk/`.
