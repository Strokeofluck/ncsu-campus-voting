Map context and its source

The article maps ballots cast at Talley in 2022 and Western Boulevard in 2026. Each age map has two circles at the university's official building coordinates. Circle area is proportional to that location's age-group ballot count, using the same size scale across all five maps: radius = 40 × √(count ÷ 4,214) pixels. Circle color identifies location and year. Counts use the matched first 16 early-voting days; they include voters from any home precinct in Wake. The growth figures below each map compare those site counts with the full county's same-age counts.

Precinct outlines are geographic context only. They do not encode voter addresses or define a site's catchment. The separate prototype maps retain the home-precinct approach: voters registered in each precinct who voted early anywhere in Wake.
