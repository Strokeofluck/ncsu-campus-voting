Map context and its source

The article’s map colors compare growth among residents recorded in each precinct with growth in the same age group countywide. This is a separate population from the polling-site counts above. Map gap = precinct growth − Wake same-age growth. Gold boundaries mark 01-23 and 01-31; they do not define the catchment of an early-voting site. Students may register at college or former home addresses under residency rules, and Wake early voters may use any site in their county.
